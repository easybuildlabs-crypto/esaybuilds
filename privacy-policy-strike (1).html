Privacy Policy for Strike

Last updated: [08-08-2026]

Strike ("the app", "we", "our") is a personal reminder and alarm app. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how it's protected.

Information we collect

Strike collects the minimum information needed to create your account and run your reminders:

  • Email address - used to create and sign in to your account, either typed directly or provided by Google Sign-In.
  • Passcode - if you sign up with email and a password instead of Google Sign-In, we never store your password itself. We store a salted SHA-256 hash of it, so the original password cannot be recovered from the stored data, even by us.
  • Reminder content - the titles, times, and status of the reminders you create.

Where your information is stored

All of the information above is stored only on your device, in a local database encrypted at rest (SQLCipher, AES-256), with the encryption key generated per-install and protected by your device's hardware Keystore. We do not operate a server or database that stores your reminders or account information - it never leaves your phone, except as described below for sign-in.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, your email address and basic profile information are provided to the app by Google's Sign-In service over an encrypted connection, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We only use this to identify your account within the app; we do not use it for advertising or share it with anyone else.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or any data unrelated to your reminders and account.

Notifications and alarms

Strike uses Android's notification and alarm system to remind you about your tasks at the times you choose. This happens entirely on your device and does not involve sending any data anywhere.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated reminders at any time from inside the app (Settings → Delete account). This immediately and irreversibly erases your account and reminder history from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

Children's privacy

Strike is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify users within the app.
