Delete Your Strike Account

Strike stores your account and all your reminders only on your device - there is no company server or database holding your information. Because of this, deleting your account is entirely something you control yourself, right on your phone, with no need to contact anyone or wait for a request to be processed.

Option 1 - Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Strike and make sure you're signed in
  2. On the dashboard, tap Delete account (top right)
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted

This immediately and permanently erases your account and every reminder stored on your device. This action cannot be undone.

Option 2 - If you've already uninstalled the app

Simply uninstalling Strike from your device already removes all locally stored data, including your account and reminders, since nothing is kept anywhere else. No further action is needed - there is no remaining copy of your data on any server to separately request deletion of.

Why there's no "submit a request" form here: Strike doesn't operate a backend server, so there's no remote account record for us to delete on your behalf - your device is the only place your data ever exists. If you have any trouble with either option above, or have questions about your data, contact us at [easybuildlabs@gmail.com].
